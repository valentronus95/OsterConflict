# S14A household dressing rules

Private homes in Oster must not look like identical showroom boxes. Public references guide the overall local character,
but individual private residences are not claimed to reproduce a specific real household unless a public, appropriate
reference is intentionally selected.

Every enterable residence gets a deterministic interior seed and a condition profile: Worn, Ordinary or Maintained.
Variation affects furniture count, small rotations, mismatched chairs, clutter density and layout choices. The content is
intentionally ordinary and inexpensive: different sofas, tables, chairs, cabinets, wardrobes, basic kitchens, fridges,
stove proxies, desktops, laptops, boxes and small household clutter.

S14A items are static dressing. They do not open inventories, contain loot, run software, provide resources or replicate
per-item state. Interaction is deferred until a later explicit gameplay requirement, preventing decorative objects from
becoming hundreds of needless replicated actors.
